Frequently asked

How do I pay?

Send your payment directly to Bechdou using one of the wallet or bank options shown at checkout, then share the transaction ID. We confirm it, QC the piece, and dispatch nationwide.

How do I know a seller is genuine?

Closets carry a verified badge once the seller has confirmed their email, and every listing is reviewed by our team for photos and honest condition notes before it goes live.

Can I sell my own clothes?

Absolutely. Tap Sell your fit, upload a few photos, set your price, and submit for approval. It's free to list.

What if the item isn't as described?

Every order is QC-checked before it is dispatched, and you can cancel yourself, free, any time before it ships. After that, message us and we will step in before the seller's payout is released.
