Frequently asked

How do I pay?

Send your payment via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or bank transfer directly to Bechdou, then share the transaction ID at checkout. We confirm it, QC the piece, and dispatch nationwide through TCS and Leopards.

How do I know a seller is genuine?

Closets carry a verified badge and a trust score. Every listing is admin-reviewed for photos and quality before it goes live.

Can I sell my own clothes?

Absolutely. Tap Sell your fit, upload a few photos, set your price, and submit for approval — it's free to list.

What if the item isn't as described?

Buyer Protection lets you decline at the door before paying. Orders are QC-checked before dispatch to avoid surprises.
